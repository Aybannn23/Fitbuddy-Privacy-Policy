Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2026

Welcome to FitBuddy! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

📱 Quick Summary: FitBuddy is designed with privacy in mind. Most of your data stays on your device. We only process AI chat messages through Google Gemini AI to provide personalized fitness advice.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device and is NOT transmitted to our servers:

  • Workout Data: Exercise logs, workout history, and fitness progress
  • Personal Preferences: App settings, workout preferences, and customization options
  • Reminder Settings: Workout reminders and notification preferences
  • Achievement Data: Unlocked achievements, progress milestones, and badges
  • Exercise Library Usage: Exercises you've viewed or marked as favorites

1.2 Information Processed by Third-Party Services

When you use the AI Chat feature:

  • Chat Messages: Your questions and conversations with the AI fitness assistant are sent to Google Gemini AI for processing
  • Context Information: Limited context about your fitness goals may be included to provide relevant advice

1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect your name, email address, or contact information
  • We do NOT track your location
  • We do NOT collect payment information (the app is free)
  • We do NOT access your device's camera or microphone
  • We do NOT collect health data from other apps or devices

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Local Data Usage

Data stored on your device is used exclusively to:

  • Display your workout history and progress
  • Track your fitness achievements and milestones
  • Provide personalized workout reminders
  • Maintain your app preferences and settings
  • Enhance your overall user experience

2.2 AI Chat Feature

When you use the AI chatbot:

  • Your messages are sent to Google Gemini AI for processing
  • Gemini AI analyzes your questions to provide fitness advice
  • Responses are generated based on fitness knowledge and best practices
  • Chat conversations are NOT stored permanently on external servers

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

All personal fitness data is stored locally on your device using:

  • SQLite Database: For workout logs and progress tracking
  • Shared Preferences: For app settings and preferences
  • Data is secured by your device's operating system security measures

3.2 Data Transmission Security

When communicating with Google Gemini AI:

  • All connections use HTTPS encryption
  • Messages are transmitted securely over encrypted channels
  • No sensitive personal information is included in AI requests

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Google Gemini AI

FitBuddy uses Google Gemini AI to power the fitness chatbot feature. When you use this feature:

  • Your chat messages are processed by Google's AI service
  • Google's privacy policy applies to this data processing
  • View Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
⚠️ Important: The AI chat feature requires an internet connection. If you prefer not to use third-party AI services, you can still use all other features of FitBuddy (exercise library, reminders, progress tracking) without the AI chat.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

Your data is shared only in these limited circumstances:

  • Google Gemini AI: Only chat messages for AI processing (as described above)
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • Safety Concerns: To protect the safety of our users or the public

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Data Control

You have full control over your data:

  • View Your Data: All data is accessible within the app
  • Delete Your Data: Uninstalling the app deletes all local data
  • Opt-Out of AI Chat: Simply don't use the AI chatbot feature
  • Disable Notifications: Turn off workout reminders in app settings

6.2 Data Portability

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • You have direct access to all your fitness data
  • Data is deleted when you uninstall the app
  • No account or cloud sync is required

7. Children's Privacy

FitBuddy is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Data Retention

  • Local Data: Stored on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app
  • AI Chat Messages: Not permanently stored; processed in real-time by Google Gemini AI
  • No Account Data: We don't maintain user accounts, so there's no account data to retain

9. International Users

FitBuddy can be used worldwide. Please note:

  • Your local data stays on your device regardless of location
  • AI chat processing may involve data transmission to Google's servers
  • Different countries have different privacy laws - please ensure compliance with local regulations

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes:

  • The "Last Updated" date at the top will be revised
  • Significant changes will be announced within the app
  • Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance
  • We encourage you to review this policy periodically

11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know: Request information about data we collect (minimal in our case)
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt-out of data "sales" (we don't sell data)
  • Non-Discrimination: We won't discriminate against you for exercising your rights

12. European Users (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of Access: Access your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Right to Object: Object to data processing

13. Security Measures

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information:

  • Local data is protected by your device's security features
  • Network communications use encryption (HTTPS)
  • We follow industry best practices for secure app development
  • Regular security updates and maintenance
⚠️ Note: While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
